# Refund policy

A refund can be requested within 7 days of delivery. The item must be unused and
in its original packaging.

Once we receive the returned item, the refund is processed in 5 to 7 working
days. The money goes back to the original payment method. UPI refunds usually
land faster than card refunds.

Opened software, gift cards and items marked final sale cannot be refunded.

If an order has not shipped yet, cancelling is faster than refunding, because
the amount is released the same day.
