# Vegetable Poha

Meal: breakfast
Diet: vegetarian, vegan
Contains: peanuts
Time: 20 minutes
Serves: 2

## Ingredients
- 2 cups poha (flattened rice)
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 potato, diced
- 1/4 cup peanuts
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 8 curry leaves
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1 green chilli
- lemon juice and coriander to finish

## Steps
1. Rinse the poha in water and drain it.
2. Fry the peanuts in oil until crunchy, then take them out.
3. Add mustard seeds, curry leaves, chilli, onion and potato. Cook until the potato is soft.
4. Add turmeric, salt and the poha. Mix gently for 2 minutes.
5. Add the peanuts back, finish with lemon juice and coriander.
