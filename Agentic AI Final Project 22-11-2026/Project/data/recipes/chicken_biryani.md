# Chicken Biryani

Meal: lunch or dinner
Diet: non-vegetarian
Contains: dairy (curd)
Time: 75 minutes
Serves: 4

## Ingredients
- 500 g chicken
- 2 cups basmati rice
- 1 cup curd
- 3 onions, sliced and fried
- 2 tsp biryani masala
- 1 tsp ginger garlic paste
- mint, coriander, saffron milk

## Steps
1. Marinate the chicken with curd, ginger garlic paste and biryani masala for 30 minutes.
2. Cook the rice until 70% done and drain.
3. Cook the marinated chicken until almost done.
4. Layer chicken, rice, fried onions, mint and saffron milk.
5. Cover and cook on low heat (dum) for 20 minutes.
