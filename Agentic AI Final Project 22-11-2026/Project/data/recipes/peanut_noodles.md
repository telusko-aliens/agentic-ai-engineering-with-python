# Thai Peanut Noodles

Meal: lunch or dinner
Diet: vegetarian, vegan
Contains: peanuts, soy, gluten
Time: 20 minutes
Serves: 2

## Ingredients
- 200 g noodles
- 3 tbsp peanut butter
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp lime juice
- 1 tsp chilli flakes
- 1 carrot, 1 capsicum, sliced
- spring onions and crushed peanuts to finish

## Steps
1. Boil the noodles and drain.
2. Whisk peanut butter, soy sauce, lime juice, chilli flakes and a little hot water.
3. Stir fry carrot and capsicum for 3 minutes.
4. Toss noodles, vegetables and sauce together.
5. Top with spring onions and crushed peanuts.
