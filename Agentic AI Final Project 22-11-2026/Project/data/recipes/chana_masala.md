# Chana Masala

Meal: lunch or dinner
Diet: vegetarian, vegan
Contains: none of the common allergens
Time: 40 minutes
Serves: 3

## Ingredients
- 1 cup chickpeas, soaked overnight
- 2 onions, 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 1 tsp ginger garlic paste
- 2 tsp chana masala powder
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- coriander and lemon to finish

## Steps
1. Pressure cook the chickpeas for 5 whistles.
2. Fry onions until brown, add ginger garlic paste.
3. Add tomatoes, turmeric and chana masala powder, cook into a thick masala.
4. Add the chickpeas with some water, simmer for 10 minutes.
5. Finish with coriander and lemon juice.
