# Dal Tadka

Meal: lunch or dinner
Diet: vegetarian, vegan (use oil instead of ghee)
Contains: none of the common allergens
Time: 30 minutes
Serves: 3

## Ingredients
- 1 cup toor dal
- 1 onion, 1 tomato, chopped
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 2 garlic cloves
- 1/2 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp red chilli powder
- 1 tbsp ghee or oil
- coriander to finish

## Steps
1. Pressure cook the dal with turmeric and salt for 3 whistles.
2. Heat ghee, add cumin and garlic until golden.
3. Add onion and tomato, cook until soft, add chilli powder.
4. Pour this tadka over the dal, simmer 5 minutes, finish with coriander.
