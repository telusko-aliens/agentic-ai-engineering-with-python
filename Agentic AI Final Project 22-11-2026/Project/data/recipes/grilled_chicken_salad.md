# Grilled Chicken Salad

Meal: lunch or dinner
Diet: non-vegetarian, high protein
Contains: none of the common allergens
Time: 25 minutes
Serves: 2

## Ingredients
- 300 g chicken breast
- 1 cucumber, 2 tomatoes, 1 onion
- lettuce
- 1 tbsp olive oil, lemon juice
- salt, pepper, oregano

## Steps
1. Season the chicken with salt, pepper and oregano.
2. Grill for 6 minutes on each side, rest and slice.
3. Chop the vegetables and toss with olive oil and lemon.
4. Top with the sliced chicken.
