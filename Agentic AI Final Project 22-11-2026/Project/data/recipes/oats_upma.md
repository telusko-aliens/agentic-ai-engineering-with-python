# Oats Upma

Meal: breakfast
Diet: vegetarian, vegan
Contains: none of the common allergens
Time: 15 minutes
Serves: 2

## Ingredients
- 1 cup rolled oats
- 1 onion, chopped
- 1 carrot, diced
- 1/4 cup green peas
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 1 green chilli
- 6 curry leaves
- coriander to finish

## Steps
1. Dry roast the oats for 3 minutes and keep aside.
2. Fry mustard seeds, curry leaves, chilli and onion in oil.
3. Add carrot and peas, cook for 4 minutes.
4. Add the oats, salt and 1 cup of water. Stir until the water is absorbed.
5. Finish with coriander and serve hot.
