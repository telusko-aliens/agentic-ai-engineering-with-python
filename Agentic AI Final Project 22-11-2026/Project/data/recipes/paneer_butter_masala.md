# Paneer Butter Masala

Meal: lunch or dinner
Diet: vegetarian (not vegan)
Contains: dairy, cashews (tree nuts)
Time: 35 minutes
Serves: 3

## Ingredients
- 250 g paneer, cubed
- 3 tomatoes, pureed
- 10 cashews, soaked and ground
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1/4 cup cream
- 1 tsp ginger garlic paste
- 1 tsp garam masala, 1 tsp red chilli powder
- 1 tsp kasuri methi

## Steps
1. Melt butter, fry ginger garlic paste for 1 minute.
2. Add tomato puree and cook for 10 minutes.
3. Add cashew paste, chilli powder and garam masala, cook 5 minutes.
4. Add paneer and cream, simmer for 5 minutes.
5. Crush kasuri methi on top and serve with roti or rice.
