# Masala Omelette

Meal: breakfast
Diet: eggetarian (not vegetarian, not vegan)
Contains: eggs
Time: 10 minutes
Serves: 1

## Ingredients
- 2 eggs
- 1 small onion, chopped
- 1 tomato, chopped
- 1 green chilli
- coriander, salt, pepper

## Steps
1. Beat the eggs with salt and pepper.
2. Mix in the onion, tomato, chilli and coriander.
3. Pour into a hot oiled pan and cook for 2 minutes on each side.
